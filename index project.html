About Chatter

Chatter is a multi-functional platform where authors and readers can have access to their own content. It aims to be a traditional bookshop's favorite shelves and more for digital readers.

Why you should join chatter

Our goal is to make writers and readers see our platform as their next heaven for blogging, ensuring ease in interactions, connecting with like-minded peers, and growing together.

Analytics

Analytics to track the number of views, likes and comment and also analyze the performance of your articles over a period of time.

Social interactions

Users on the platform can interact with posts they like, comment and engage in discussions.

Content creation

Write nice and appealing with our well-structured reading and writing tools.
